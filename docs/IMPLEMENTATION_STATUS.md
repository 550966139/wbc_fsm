- Regression after the changes: `ctest` **5/5**, `run-port-tests.sh` **210 + 25 + 51 checks, 0 failures** (224, fresh `build-tests` tree + full `build`).

Two deployment fixes were required to make the trained policy enterable (both discovered in sim, neither a training-contract mismatch):

1. **Safety guard calibration** (`config/g1_23dof.json`): `max_target_step` 0.35 → 1.0, `max_tracking_error` 0.8 → 1.2. Offline ONNX replay at the exact entry observation showed the policy's intrinsic first-cycle step is ≈ 0.36 rad from the training-default pose and up to ≈ 0.8 rad with measured offsets (action scale 0.44–0.55 with actions ≈ ±1.5–1.9 on the shoulders), so the original guards rejected the policy on every entry. These guards are port-authored values, not upstream hardware limits; recalibrate again if the policy is retrained.
2. **Target clamping** (`g1::clampTargets23` in `include/common/policy23.h`, applied in `State_Policy23::run` and the 23DoF branch of `State_FixedStand::run`): a few cycles after entry, closed-loop dynamics drove shoulder/ankle targets past configured joint limits and the old throw-to-damping behavior aborted the run. Training never sees this because MuJoCo saturates position targets at ctrlrange; the clamp reproduces that. This also removes the earlier "1 mrad XML shrink" real-robot concern for stand recovery (scene shrink remains harmless).

Known sim-only caveats: with the elastic band attached at 0.9 m the band unloads ≈ 75 % of the robot's weight and walking degrades — validate locomotion only with the band off; do not press Backspace in the viewer while the controller runs (tick rollback looks like a long state outage to the IOSDK layer).

## Stand-gain restoration for FixedStand (2026-09-18 afternoon)

Root cause of "START does not stand" from the first attended hardware session, then reproduced in sim: the 23DoF port made `State_FixedStand` reuse the policy-contract gains (legs 40.2/99.1/28.5, tau=0). Upstream 29DoF stand used its own gains (`State_FixedStand.h`: hips 100, knee 150, ankles 40, waist 300) and assumed the robot entered already standing ("Please make the robot stand first"). With contract gains a sling-hung robot is not pulled into stance (ankle sag ≈0.245 rad blocked the 0.15 rad policy-entry gate; sling support was the missing physical premise).

- Config: optional `stand_stiffness`/`stand_damping` (23 values each, mapped from the authored 29DoF gains by joint name: hips 100/100/100, knee 150, ankles 40, waist 300, arms 100/100/50/50/20; kd legs 2/2/2/4/2/2, waist 3, arms 2/2/2/2/1). ## Policy-chain sim re-pass fixes (2026-09-22, agent-5 findings on 224)

The 224 sim2sim re-pass of the dance↔walk chain exposed two defects, both fixed **without relaxing any threshold**:

**Problem A — motion-end handoff was one-shot.** Observed: `policy entry rejected: worst joint 15 error 0.491851 rad, max |dq| 1.91259; returning to fixed stand` — post-dance settling jitter (|dq| briefly >1.0) plus residual error failed the single-cycle gate, 0/3 handoffs succeeded (each fell back to FixedStand safely), while a manual R2+A ~15 s later (after settling) entered the policy — proving the gate only needs to wait for settling. Fix (**settle-hold window**): on motion completion the motion cursor freezes on the last (stance outro) frame and the tracking policy keeps running; `checkChange` re-evaluates the policy entry gate every cycle, hands off to LOCO the first cycle it passes, and falls back to FIXEDSTAND with the reason line only after `handoff_settle_timeout` (new config key, default 3.0 s, strict-parsed in `config_tracking23.h` and validated by `--check`). The R2+B abort path gets **no** window (a mid-dance pose cannot settle into the gate): one-shot evaluation, pass → LOCO, fail → FIXEDSTAND — unchanged semantics.

**Problem B — target-step guard blind to entry/load transients (previous seeding).** Observed: E2@7000 (step guard 0.6, calibrated from ideal replay ×1.5) failed four band-detached runs, all entry/load-switch transients: walk→dance joint 10 step 0.68 (walk-policy ankle/knee commands reach 0.9+, colliding with dance frame-0 targets), band-detached stand→dance step 0.95, mid-dance load-change step 0.73. E1@3000's 1.0 guard happened to absorb the same transients (full band-detached pass), so this was the calibration protocol's blind spot, not a policy regression. Fix: on every FSM state transition (`FSM::run`, after `enter()`) `CtrlComponents::noteStateEntry()` makes `validateTargets23` seed `previous` from the **current measured pose** for exactly the first active control cycle instead of inheriting the previous state's last command; later cycles keep the command chain. Entry gates already bound |q − entry pose|, so the first-cycle step is the true command jump. Applies to all three 23DoF states (TRACKING/LOCO/FixedStand) and only to their first cycle; the post-damping path already seeded from measured (`previousActive_ = false`) and is unchanged.

Equivalence analysis (walk/stand paths): after damping, behaviour is identical (already measured-seeded). FixedStand→LOCO (R2+A) and TRACKING→LOCO handoff: old basis |target − oldStateLastTarget| vs new |target − measured q|; the old state converged to the entry pose and the gate bounds |q − defaults| ≤ 0.15, so the bases differ by ≤ 0.15 rad per joint (triangle inequality) against a 1.0 guard over a 0.36–0.8 intrinsic first-cycle envelope — equivalent within the gate envelope. TRACKING entry (R2+B): the old basis could reach ~0.9+ (walk ankle commands) and false-trip; the new basis |danceTarget − q| with q within 0.15 of frame 0 is strictly the physical command jump — the E2 signature is fixed at the source. FixedStand entries: new basis ≈ blend × |defaults − q| ≈ 0 — strictly tighter, never looser. Guard calibrations (replay-derived from defaults) transfer because entry gates bind q to the entry pose.
